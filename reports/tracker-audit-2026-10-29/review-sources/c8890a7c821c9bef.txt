 Cyprus Granfondo SUMATOR Seznam Mapa + Vložit závod Přihlásit se Seznam závodů › Cyprus Granfondo Silnice Cyprus Granfondo UCI Gran Fondo World Series Datum 4.4.2025 - 6.4.2025 Pá–Ne Místo Paphos (Cyprus) Cyprus Gran Fondo has been held for 10 years. The event will again be part of the UCI Gran Fondo World Series in a three days stage race in and around the city of Pafos on the west end of the island. Almost 80% of the participants come from abroad to combine the cycling event with some tourism and training opportunities on the sunny island when it’s still pretty cold in the rest of Europe.
