 Granfondo Novi Sad SUMATOR Seznam Mapa + Vložit závod Přihlásit se Seznam závodů › Granfondo Novi Sad Silnice Granfondo Novi Sad UCI Gran Fondo World Series Datum 7.6.2026 Neděle Místo Novi Sad (Serbia) The Gran Fondo Novi Sad was held for the first time as part of the UCI Gran Fondo World Series in 2024. Its route leads through the beautiful landscapes of the Fruška gora National Park, starting and ending in the city of Novi Sad, which held the title of European Capital of Culture 2022. Gran Fondo Novi Sad has two race distances, both with start and finish in Novi Sad. The races will start on Sunday morning in front of the Provincial Government building in the city centre of Novi Sad.
