## Recommendations (priority order)
1. **High: connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster (free)** to obtain real referring domains and links. Add a free Moz key (2,500 rows/month) to enable Tier 1. Re-run in 2-3 months and check the next CC release.
2. **High: organizer "Listed on Let's Race" badge and embeddable widget** (next-race list or single race card, iframe or script) on the race page and in the organizer flow. The widget should include a followed, keyword-neutral link to the race URL. It earns links from organizer sites, which are the natural linkers of an event directory.
3. **High: federation and club partnerships.** Offer a calendar feed or widget to the Czech cycling federation (ceskysvazcyklistiky.cz), Slovak, Polish and German federations and regional clubs, in return for a "calendar partner" link. Pitch a data feed (iCal/JSON) to local cycling clubs.
4. Medium: race-organizer outreach on the 79 series pages (one series owner links to one page, which is a simple ask). Add a "claim this race" flow that ends with a backlink request.
5. Medium: linkable data assets such as an annual "Central Europe cycling race calendar" report and country-level stats. Pitch them to Czech cycling media and blogs.
6. Medium: submit to directories and aggregators (Strava clubs, Wikipedia "external links" where editorially fair, cycling forums). Keep anchors branded and natural. No paid or bulk link schemes.
7. Low: for crawl discovery, make sure inbound internal links and the sitemap work. This is covered by the technical audit. Recommend `/seo technical https://letsrace.cz`.
