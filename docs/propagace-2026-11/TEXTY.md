### Indie Hackers / r/SideProject

**Titulek:** I built a map of every cycling race in Central Europe

> Hi! I'm a [hobby cyclist / parent of kids who race] from Czechia. Finding races here meant checking a dozen federation calendars, series websites and Facebook pages, each in a different language and format.
>
> So I built Let's Race: one map and one list of road, MTB, gravel, cyclocross and kids' races across seven countries. Today it has over 900 races for 2027 and it fills up as organisers announce new dates.
>
> A few things I'm happy with:
> - Filters for kids' age categories, so parents can find races their child can actually enter.
> - Save races to a plan and subscribe to it from your phone's calendar; date changes come through on their own.
> - Most of the catalogue is kept up to date automatically: readers check federation calendars and organisers' pages every day and pull dates and categories from the regulations.
>
> https://letsrace.cz/en
>
> I'd love feedback, especially from anyone who races in the region. What's missing?
