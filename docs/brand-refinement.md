# Brand refinement — branch proposal

This is a local design proposal on `codex/brand-refinement`, based on the
existing Let's Race wordmark and Stone palette.

## Visual rules

- Keep Geist, the white page surface and the compact application shell.
- Brand red (`--brand`, `#c81d25`) identifies the wordmark and active navigation.
- Discipline colours retain their existing meaning across map pins and browse
  icons. They are data cues, not additional brand colours.
- Browse pages have no decorative photos, ribbons or divider lines. Use spacing
  and typography to separate the introduction and sections.
- Country cards use flags. Discipline and series cards remain compact text
  links with genuine catalogue counts.
- Card hover and keyboard focus use neutral black and grey, including chevrons.
- Keep the logo bar at 48px across map, list, calendar and browse.
- Countries, disciplines and series share intro typography and card spacing.
- Discipline cards follow the filter order in every country: MTB, road, gravel,
  cyclocross, track, BMX. Families without catalogue races remain omitted.
- On phones, keep cards compact, wrap labels and retain bottom tabs.
- Loading states use the same layout and card dimensions as loaded pages.
- The original OG and welcome photographs remain in their existing locations.

The generated road-image exploration and the browse-photo placement were
rejected and are not part of this proposal.
